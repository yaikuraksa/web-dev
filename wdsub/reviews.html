❤ Rom-Com Reviews ❤

Welcome to my reviews of my top five romance-comedies! There is a perfect 1990s rom-com for everyone, so enjoy!

Ten Things I Hate About You

10 Things I Hate About You is by far the best rom-com to exist. It encompasses the true feelings girls and boys go through, while keeping the mood light and silly. I personally really enjoy that they can make jokes without being called out for being rude and that all the characters are well-developed.

When Harry Met Sally

Another amazing rom-com, with older characters than 10 Things I Hate About You, is When Harry Met Sally. It follows the story of a man and woman who meet early on and do not realize they are made for each other until they run into each other often. It really ties in silly humor, my favorite, and makes it clear that if you meet somebody often, maybe you were meant to be together. I love their jokes and I look for someone with the same persistence is Harry.

Me Before You

An unlikely story unfolds between two very opposite characters in Me Before You, making it a heart-warming and charming watch. It is easy to fall in love with the female lead and it is espcially easy to cry at the end. This movie really touched my emotions and made e realize that if someone truly loves you, they will know exactly what you like.

While You Were Sleeping

If you want to watch a heart-warming, yet surprising, story about a woman and her coma crush, While You Were Sleeping is perfect for you! I personally enjoyed the ending, which ties the movie into a perfect happy ending. It has a warm, welcoming vibe and is perfect for a cozy movie night. Though the female lead, Sandra Bullock, is not my favorite actress, it is a great movie nonetheless.

How to Lose a Guy in Ten Days

In How to Lose a Guy in Ten Days, after betting to end a ten-day date in different ways, the characters unexpectedly fall in love. My favorite rom-coms include unexpected love, so I especially loved the plot in this one. The female lead is very lovable and sweet, someone who achieves their goals as well, and that is something I admire. I love that the male lead chases the girl down, just how all great rom-coms should end.
