Iaryna's Half-Brownie-Half-Cookie Recipe

Make the best "brookies"in town!

  1. Gather Your Ingredients

    • 4 cups all-purpose flour
    • 2 cups white sugar
    • 1 cup brown sugar
    • 2 tsp baking soda
    • 2 eggs
    • 1 and 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
    • 1 cup cocoa powder
    • 1 and 1/2 cup chocolate chips (optional)
    • 1 cup softened butter
    • 2 pinches of salt

  2. Prepare Your Kitchen

    • Preheat your oven to 375 degrees F
    • Gather two large bowls
    • Gather two medium bowls
    • Cover 2-inch-deep brownie pan with baking spray or a baking sheet
    • Place butter in room-temperature area to soften

  3. Combine the Wet Ingredients

    1. Cookie Mix

      1. In a large bowl, whisk 1 cup brown sugar, 1 cup white sugar, 1/2 cup softened butter, 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, 1/2 cup softened butter
      2. Beat in 1 egg until smooth
      3. Set aside

    2. Brownie Mix

      1. In another large bowl, whisk 1 cup white sugar, 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
      2. Beat in 1 egg until smooth
      3. Set aside

  4. Combine the Dry Ingredients

    1. Cookie Mix

      1. In a medium bowl, sift and whisk 2 cups flour, 1 teaspoon baking soda, and a pinch of salt
      2. Add the dry cookie mixture in thirds to the wet cookie mixture
      3. (Optional) Fold in 1 and 1/2 cup chocolate chips
      4. Set aside

    2. Brownie Mix

      1. In another medium bowl, sift and whisk 2 cups of flour, 1 teaspoon baking soda, 1 cup cocoa powder, and another pinch of salt
      2. Add the dry brownie mixture to the wet brownie mixture

  5. Prepare to Bake

    1. Fill brownie pan with brownie mix and spread evenly
    2. On top of the brownie mix, spread the cookie mix evenly
    3. Tap lightly on a hard surface to fill any space and remove air bubbles
    4. Bake for 20 minutes

  6. Enjoy!

    • Remove from oven and cool for 20 minutes
    • Serve with ice cream, milk, and other add-ins!
